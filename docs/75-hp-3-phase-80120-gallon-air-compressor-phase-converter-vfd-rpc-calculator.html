Frequently Asked Questions (7.5 HP 3-Phase 80/120-Gallon Air Compressor Phase Converter)

Why do I need a 15 HP VFD to run a 7.5 HP 3-phase motor on single-phase 240V?

When a standard 3-phase VFD is fed by 240V single-phase power (connected to L1 and L2), input current increases by $\sqrt{3}$ (1.732x)—rising from 22.0A to 40.7A—while flowing through only 4 of the 6 input rectifier diodes. Upsizing to a 15 HP (11.2 kW) VFD prevents diode overheating and DC bus undervoltage faults.

Should I use a VFD or a 15 HP Rotary Phase Converter for 7.5 HP 3-Phase 80/120-Gallon Air Compressor Phase Converter?

Use a VFD (15 HP) if you want dial-controlled variable spindle RPM, soft-start acceleration, and electronic dynamic braking on a single motor. Use a 15 HP Rotary Phase Converter if you are powering a high-inrush reciprocating air compressor, a machine with complex 3-phase transformers/coolant pumps, or multiple 3-phase tools simultaneously.

What size 240V breaker and wire do I need for 7.5 HP 3-Phase 80/120-Gallon Air Compressor Phase Converter?

Drawing 40.7 Amps on the 240V single-phase input legs, NEC Article 430.122 (125%–135% input rating) requires a dedicated 70-Amp 2-pole circuit breaker wired with 4 AWG THHN Copper in Conduit.
